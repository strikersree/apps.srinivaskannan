Why Divya Prabhandham

  • Works Offline The full text, translations, and scripts are bundled in the app — read on a flight, in a temple, or anywhere without signal.
  • Never Lose Your Place Reading position and bookmarks are saved automatically and sync across your devices via iCloud or your Google Account.
  • Read It Your Way Pick your script and language, reading font, accent color, and light, dark, or high-contrast appearance.
  • Listen Along Play accompanying pasurams through Apple Music, Amazon Music, or YouTube Music while you follow the text.
