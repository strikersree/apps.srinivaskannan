Privacy Policy — Divya Prabhandham

Effective date: 5th September 2026

Publisher: Srinivas Kannan

Contact: srinivas.kannan@icloud.com

Divya Prabhandham ("the App") is a reader and audio player for the Naalayira Divyaprabandham. This policy explains how the App handles information across iOS, iPadOS, and Android platforms. In short, we (the publisher) do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. The App displays ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device and advertising identifiers as described below; core reading features continue to work fully offline regardless of your ad settings.

Summary

  • We (the publisher) do not collect or store any personal data from you on our own servers.
  • Core app content (text, translations, and scripts) is bundled inside the App and works without an internet connection.
  • Your reading data and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Optional cloud sync relies strictly on platform-native accounts (iCloud on iOS/iPadOS or Google Drive/Play Services on Android), managed by Apple and Google respectively. We have no access to it.
  • Audio playback uses third-party music providers (Apple Music on iOS; Amazon Music or YouTube Music on Android).
  • Notifications, if enabled, are scheduled locally on your device.
  • The App displays ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve, measure, and prevent fraud in ads. The publisher does not receive or have access to this data.

Information the App Handles Locally

The App stores the following on your device to remember your place and preferences:

  • Reading data — your current reading position, bookmarks, and recently viewed sections.
  • Preferences — your chosen script/language, reading font, accent color, appearance (light/dark/contrast), and font size.
  • Reminder settings — whether daily reminders are enabled and your selected time slots.

This information stays on your device unless you choose to sync it via platform cloud services (see below). We never receive or store it.

Cloud Sync (Optional)

  • iOS / iPadOS (iCloud Sync): If enabled, your bookmarks, reading position, and preferences synchronize across your Apple devices using Apple's iCloud Key-Value storage, tied to your personal Apple Account. Managed under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Android (Google Drive / Play Services Sync): If enabled, app preferences and reading history sync using your Google Account, including your Google account email/profile identifier to associate the synced data with your account. Managed under Google's Privacy Policy.

The publisher cannot view, access, or retrieve any synced cloud data on either platform. You can disable sync at any time in the App's Settings or via your device's operating system settings.

Account Authentication (Optional)

The App offers optional single sign-in to support cross-device features:

  • Sign in with Apple (iOS / iPadOS): Stores the user identifier and name provided by Apple locally on your device. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In (Android): Stores your basic profile identifier and email, provided by Google, locally on your device for the purpose of enabling bookmark and preference sync. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Authentication details are never transmitted to the publisher or any third-party marketing servers, and are not shared with any third party. You can sign out at any time in the App's Settings.

Music & Audio Providers (Optional)

The App integrates with platform-specific music services to provide accompanying audio pasurams:

  • iOS / iPadOS — Apple Music: Requires an active Apple Music subscription to stream full audio tracks. Audio playback is handled natively via MusicKit / Apple Music API. The App requests permission to interact with your Apple Music account. The App does not collect, track, or log your music listening history. Handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Android — Amazon Music / YouTube Music: You may choose either provider for accompanying audio playback. An active subscription is not required for standard streaming; however, an Amazon Music Unlimited or YouTube Music / YouTube Premium subscription may be required by Amazon or Google, respectively, for background audio playback when the app is minimized or the screen is turned off. Audio playback is streamed via the Amazon Music or YouTube Music / Google API services. The App does not collect, track, or store your Amazon Music or YouTube playback history or personal Amazon or YouTube credentials. Handled under Amazon's or Google's Privacy Policy, respectively.

Notifications (Optional)

If enabled, the App schedules local notifications directly on your device. These are generated strictly on-device by iOS/Android system schedulers — no reminder schedules, content, or device tokens are sent to our servers. You can toggle reminders in the App's Settings or via system notification settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads through Google AdMob, a third-party advertising network operated by Google. To serve and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud, AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as your Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission).
  • IP address and general device information (device model, operating system version).
  • App usage data related to ad requests, impressions, and interactions.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address, used for ad relevance and fraud prevention — the App itself does not request or access your precise GPS location.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the publisher. The publisher does not receive or have access to any of this advertising data. Ad serving is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • iOS: You will be asked for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before any cross-app/cross-site tracking occurs. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google Settings → Ads.
  • Where required by applicable law (for example, in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before showing personalized ads or using non-essential identifiers. You may withdraw or change your consent at any time via the in-app privacy options.

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly serve personalized ads to children.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Beyond the advertising identifiers described above, the App does not collect, use, or share:

  • Personal identifiers, contact lists, or precise/coarse location data.
  • User analytics, usage profiling, or behavioral tracking data beyond what is needed for ad delivery and measurement.
  • Search queries, bookmarked pasurams, or reading habits.

The App operates without a custom backend server and makes no network requests to the publisher.

Information Platform Owners May Receive

When you download or purchase apps from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store:

  • Apple or Google may process device metadata, download metrics, and diagnostic crash logs under their respective privacy policies.
  • If you opt in to share developer diagnostics on your device, the publisher receives only aggregated, anonymized crash reports and basic app usage statistics via App Store Connect and Google Play Console. This data is entirely decoupled from your personal identity.

Platform Privacy Declarations

Because the App now serves ads via Google AdMob, its official declarations reflect data collected on Google's behalf for advertising purposes:

  • Apple App Store: The app's Privacy Nutrition Label declares identifiers and usage data used for Advertising, as required by Apple. See the App Store listing for the current declaration.
  • Google Play Data Safety: Declares that device or other identifiers are collected and shared with Google for advertising purposes. See the Play Store listing's Data Safety section for the current declaration.

The publisher's own data handling, described throughout this policy, remains unchanged: we do not operate servers that collect, sell, or share your personal data.

Children's Privacy

The App is family-friendly and suitable for all ages, but it is not directed specifically at children under 13. The publisher does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly serve personalized ads to children — see Advertising above. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because all personal configurations reside on your device or personal cloud storage:

  • Local Data: Uninstalling the App instantly deletes all local data from your device.
  • Synced Cloud Data: Disable cloud sync in the App's Settings, or delete stored app data via iOS Settings (iCloud) or Android Settings (Google Drive / App Management).
  • Account Sign-Out: Tap Sign Out in the App's Settings to remove Apple or Google sign-in tokens from local device storage.
  • Advertising Data: Advertising identifiers are controlled by your device's operating system settings (see Advertising above) — the publisher holds no such data to delete.

Security

Data stored on your device relies on the hardware-level encryption and security architecture of iOS/Android. Cloud data relies on Apple and Google cloud security infrastructure. Because we operate no developer databases or remote servers, there is no remote database to breach.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect app enhancements or platform regulatory requirements. Updates will be indicated by revising the effective date at the top of this policy and publishing the revised version on the App Store and Google Play Store listings, and at https://strikersree.github.io/apps.srinivaskannan/divya-prabhandham/privacy.html.
