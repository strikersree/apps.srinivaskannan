# Privacy Policy — Divya Prabhandham

**Effective date:** 19th July 2026
**Publisher:** SRINIVAS KANNAN
**Contact:** srinivas.kannan@icloud.com

Divya Prabhandham ("the App") is a reader for the Naalayira Divya Prabandham. This policy explains how the App handles information. In short: **the App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.** It has no accounts on our servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. It works fully offline.

## Summary

- We (the publisher) do **not** collect any personal data from you.
- All of the App's content is bundled in the App and works without a network connection.
- Your reading data and preferences are stored **locally on your device**.
- If you turn on iCloud sync, that data syncs through **your own iCloud account**, managed by Apple. We have no access to it.
- Notifications, if you enable them, are scheduled locally on your device.

## Information the App handles

The App stores the following **on your device** so it can remember your place and preferences:

- **Reading data** — your current reading position, bookmarks, and recently viewed sections.
- **Preferences** — your chosen script/language, reading font, accent color, appearance (light/dark/contrast), and font size.
- **Reminder settings** — whether daily reminders are on and the times you selected.

This information stays on your device unless you enable iCloud sync (see below). We never receive it.

## iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable **iCloud Save**, your bookmarks, reading position, and preferences are synchronized across your devices using Apple's iCloud Key-Value storage, tied to **your personal Apple Account**. This data is handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The publisher of this App cannot see, access, or retrieve it. You can turn iCloud Save off at any time in the App's Settings, or disable iCloud for the App in the iOS Settings app.

## Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Apple, the App stores the identifier and name provided by Apple **locally on your device**, to support account-related features. This information is **not** sent to the publisher or any third party. You can sign out at any time in the App's Settings. Sign in with Apple is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Notifications (optional)

If you enable daily reminders, the App schedules **local notifications** on your device at the times you choose. These are generated on-device by iOS; no reminder content or schedule is sent to us or to any server. You can turn reminders off in the App's Settings, or disable notifications for the App in the iOS Settings app.

## Information we do NOT collect

The App does **not** collect, use, or share:

- Personal identifiers, contact information, or precise or coarse location.
- Analytics, usage statistics, or behavioral data.
- Advertising identifiers; the App shows no ads and contains no ad or tracking SDKs.
- Any content you read, bookmark, or search within the App.

The App makes no network requests to the publisher and has no backend servers.

## Information Apple may receive

When you download or use an app from the App Store or TestFlight, Apple may process certain information (such as download counts and, if you choose to share diagnostics with developers, crash logs) under Apple's own policies. If enabled, the publisher may receive only Apple's **aggregated, anonymized** App Analytics and crash reports through App Store Connect. This information is not linked to your identity. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## App Store privacy label

Consistent with this policy, the App's App Store privacy label is **"Data Not Collected."**

## Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, no special data-handling for children applies.

## Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device (and, optionally, in your own iCloud):

- **Delete local data:** uninstall the App to remove all data stored on the device.
- **Remove synced data:** turn off iCloud Save in the App, or manage/delete the App's iCloud data through the iOS Settings app under your Apple Account.
- **Sign out:** use Sign Out in the App's Settings to remove the stored Apple sign-in details from the device.

## Security

Data stored on your device is protected by iOS's built-in security. iCloud data is protected by Apple's iCloud security. Because we hold none of your data on any server, there is no server-side store of your information to be breached.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above and, where appropriate, within the App or its App Store listing.
